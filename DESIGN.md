# Radio Remedy design
Preserve the supplied orange radio artwork, charcoal background, lime actions, warm white type, original members, recordings, repertoire and external destinations. Artist identity and listening lead; contact and official video are secondary journeys. No new photography or invented content.

Existing tokens in assets/site.css are canonical: background #121315, panel #1c1e21, text #f5f0e6, muted #bcbab5, accent #e8ff77, divider #45474a. Arial/Helvetica, restrained 3–4px control radii, 44px minimum action targets, responsive editorial sections. Reduced motion remains supported.

References: Bandcamp (https://bandcamp.com/) for artist-first listening hierarchy; WCAG 2.2 (https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) for semantic status, focus and reflow. These are pattern references, not copied assets.
